1. RULES OF THE CANDIDATE (plain words)  -  flow_bigprint_oiup_30s
 a) Burst: at second s, Binance BTCUSDT moved >= 8 x vol in the last 30 seconds (last trade price of second s-1 vs
    s-31; vol = btc_common Market.vol).  After a burst no new burst is taken for 900 s.  Direction = burst direction.
 b) Big print: the largest single aggressive trade IN the burst direction during the last 60 s (Binance mb / ms) is
    > 30 % of the largest aggressive trade (either side) of the last hour.
 c) Open interest did not fall: the 5-min bucket that holds the burst (create_time ct = start of that bucket) shows
    Binance open interest up >= +0.05 % vs the previous row.  This row is known only at ct + 360 s, so the decision is
    taken at ct + 360 s (60-360 s after the burst), market order, +2 s latency.
 d) Exit: stop 10 x vol (vol at the decision second), target 3R, otherwise out after 2 hours.  Max 2 positions
    (in practice they never overlapped more than that).
 Order of magnitude: stop ~$350, ~11 trades a month, average time in trade 75 min.
 flow_info_60s_wide: same rules with a 60-s window (>= 8 x vol in 60 s), open interest up >= +0.15 %, stop 20 x vol,
 no target, out after 2 hours, max 2 positions.

2. WHAT I TRIED FOR THE GIVEN ANGLE (all honest timing) AND WHY IT FAILED
 * With the corrected timing the open-interest-drop continuation is gone: after buckets with OI <= -0.5 % and move
   >= 0.3 % the price drifts +0.0 to +0.3 vol over the next 25 min (median slightly negative); the pre-correction
   "push" was the look-ahead.  Following or fading such buckets loses after costs at every hold from 5 min to 4 h.
 * Detecting the liquidation bucket in real time from 1-second flow: contemporaneous AUC for OI drop is at best 0.76
   (volume intensity over the bucket), ~0.67 when only earlier data is used; precision at the top decile 12-18 %.
   Entering on such predictions loses (false positives cost ~2 spreads each).
 * Gradient boosting on ~18,000 bursts (90 flow / depth / premium / OI-history features, train one half, test the
   other): top-decile 5-min forward move +0.9 to +1.2 vol, below the ~1.3 vol round-trip cost (2 Exness spreads).
 * Binance -> Exness lead-lag: correlation of 1-s changes 0.87 at lag 0, 0.14 at lag 1, ~0 from 2 s (our latency).
   Exness quotes overshoot fast moves and give back 0.2-0.4 vol within 10 s - smaller than the cost.
 * "Catch it faster" on the winning signal: entering at the burst itself and cutting at ct+360 if open interest did
   not confirm gives -47R (330 trades; the 261 unconfirmed lose -65R, the 69 confirmed make only +17.8R vs +54.2R
   when entered at ct+360).  Bursts retrace in the first minutes; waiting for the open interest also buys a better
   price.  So faster entry is clearly worse here.
 * Bursts with a small relative print (< 30 %) lose in almost every burst definition; OI-drop bursts faded or
   followed for 0.5-4 h lose.

7. HONEST VERDICT
 * The open-interest-drop / liquidation-cascade edge does not survive honest timing, and nothing in the 1-second
   flow, depth or premium let me catch liquidations early enough to pay 2 spreads.  Trying to enter faster made the
   working signal much worse.
 * What survives is a different, plausible effect: a sharp 30-s burst led by a large aggressive order while open
   interest is not falling (positions being opened, not liquidated) continues for ~2 h.  It is positive in every
   month, for longs and shorts, on May-Jun after a Jan-Apr selection, and beats the luck check.
 * Risks: only 69 trades; the exact parameter point is near the best of its neighbourhood (expect less - a fair
   guess is half the R); drawdown -32 % at 10 % risk is close to the limit and can be deeper on unseen months;
   Jan-Jun 2026 was a strongly trending market (BTC -35 % with a +12 % April), and 2-hour momentum trades may do worse
   in a range.  Exness spread changes hit it less than the 5-min ideas (stop ~$350, stress PF 2.7).
 * Practical: backtest() loads btc_common.Market(start - 32 days, end); for Jan-Jun that is btc_common's own loader
   over 7 months (~6-7 GB peak on this machine).  Monthly chunks give the same trades except a trade still open at a
   chunk end, which is closed at the chunk's last quote.
