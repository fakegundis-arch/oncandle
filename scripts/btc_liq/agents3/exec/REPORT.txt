Everything I did before the Nov 9 correction used rows from ct+60 s (look-ahead) and is thrown away.  With the honest rule

3. THE CANDIDATES (strategy.py CANDIDATES; both are the same idea and differ only in the settings shown)
 exec_flushfade_4v (main candidate):
   Every 5 minutes, when a metrics row becomes known (ct+360 s): if Binance open interest fell >= 1 % over the last three
   5-min rows (15 minutes, ct-600 .. ct+300) and the Binance price moved >= 0.4 % over the same 15 minutes, FADE that move.
   Place a limit order 4 x vol further in the direction of the flush (sell above after an up-flush, buy below after a
   down-flush).  It lives 20 minutes; if the flush does not stretch that far, nothing is traded.  The stop is 0.75 x the
   15-minute move in $, fixed from the fill.  The target is 4R.  The position is closed after 4 hours.  Up to 3 positions
   at once (consecutive flush rows can add), 10 % risk each, 30 % cap.
 exec_flushfade_3v_be: the same, but the move must be >= 0.6 %, the limit is 3 x vol away, and the stop moves to
   break-even after +1R.
 (A market order at the known time LOSES with the same signal: -8.0R.  The edge comes from the execution: wait for the flush
  to overshoot, then fade it with a stop that scales with the flush and a long timer.)

4. HONEST VERDICT
 * The v1 / v2 idea (follow the move after an OI drop) has no honest edge.  1.1 million trade-engineering variants of it
   do not beat the fake signals.  The best real setting (exec_oidrop_pullback in strategy.REJECTED: +15.8R, $3,804) is
   luck level.
 * One honest effect survives: fading a large deleveraging flush, but only with overshoot limit entries, a flush-sized stop
   and a 4-hour timer.  It is coherent (forced selling or buying overshoots, then reverts; fading moves where OI rises loses
   badly), it holds in May-Jun after a Jan-Apr choice, and it beats every fake in its own search and in the fixed-rule test.
 * Reasons for caution:
   (1) I found this family after looking at Jan-Jun with honest timing (stages A and B and the event studies).  Against the
       union of ALL my fake searches, the best fake reached +33.0R (stage A, fake 3, $2,710), only 1.6R below
       exec_flushfade_4v's +34.6R.  In $ the real result is still clearly ahead of every fake ($14,394 vs $10,044).
   (2) The sample is small: about 11 trades per month from roughly 40-50 separate flush episodes.  January and June are
       weak, and 4-hour holds make R noisy.
   (3) The in-sample drawdown is about -30 % at 10 % risk.  On unseen months worse is likely, and -35 % or deeper is
       quite possible.
   (4) Lots reach 9.6 BTC on a $14k account (about 48x notional).  This is within the cap, but real Exness fills and
       overnight swap (not modelled) would cost something on 4-hour holds.
   My expectation for unseen months: modestly positive if flushes of >= 1 % OI in 15 min keep occurring.  It is not a
   sure thing, and I would run it at reduced risk first.
