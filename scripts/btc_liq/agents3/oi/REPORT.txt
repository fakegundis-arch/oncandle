VERSION 3 - RESEARCHER "oi" (THE POSITIONING SIGNAL) - REPORT (Nov 9 2026)

0. THE MAIN FINDING: THE OLD METRICS TIMING LOOKED INTO THE FUTURE
  The metrics row with create_time ct covers the 5 minutes AFTER ct, not before.
  * Its taker long/short volume ratio matches Binance 1-second taker volume in [ct, ct+300) with correlation 0.994.
    Over [ct-300, ct) the correlation is 0.098.
  * Its open-interest change lines up with price and volume activity in [ct, ct+300), not in [ct-300, ct).
  * Binance 1-second and Exness 1-second prices line up at lag 0 (correlation 0.87).
  Using a row from ct+60 therefore looked about 4 minutes ahead.
  Versions 1 and 2, rebuilt with my code: under the old timing, 51 trades +17.4R and 94 trades +20.9R. With rows used
  only from ct+360 they lose: v1 PF 0.86, -3.4R; v2 PF 0.95, -1.5R.
  The lead confirmed this, and btc_common now sets known_ts = ct + 360. Everything below uses the honest timing.

1. CANDIDATE (strategy.py, CANDIDATES = {"OI_BIGMOVE": ...}; maxpos 1)
  The rule, in plain words. When a metrics row becomes known (ct + 360 s), trade if both hold:
    - Binance open interest fell at least 0.4 % over the 30 minutes ending with that row;
    - the Binance price moved at least 0.8 % during that row's 5 minutes [ct, ct+300).
  Then follow the move with a market order. Stop 4 x vol, target 1.5 R. Once the trade is +1 R ahead, the stop moves
  to break-even and then trails 0.5 R behind the best price. Exit after 10 minutes at the latest. One position at a
  time; maxpos 2 or 3 gives identical results.
  Numbers below are from strategy.backtest + btc_common.account, $1,000 at 10 % risk. They were run in 2-month chunks
  to save memory, and match the search engine exactly.

    May-Jun        15     +2.5   1.42   53 %     1,184    -25 %     0.72 BTC     1.26 (+1.7R, $1,088)

  Per-month R: Jan +2.0 (8 trades), Feb +15.2 (29), Mar +1.0 (7), Apr -0.6 (2), May -0.5 (1), Jun +3.0 (14).

2. SEARCH (honest timing, all fills / exits through btc_common.nb_trades and nb_account, checked against them)
  Candidate pool: 9,102 buckets from Jan to Jun with an open-interest drop on some window, or a big rise.
  For every bucket, both directions and 840 exits were precomputed:
    stop 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 x vol; target 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 R or none; hold 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 60 / 120 min;
    trail: none, BE 1R + trail 1R, BE at 0.5R, BE 1R + trail 0.5R.
  The search crossed these settings:
    - 34 open-interest conditions: 5 / 10 / 15 / 30-minute drops, a z-score against the last 7 days, and rises;
    - 10 move conditions: |5-min| or |15-min| move;
    - follow or fade;
    - one extra filter (or none), 450 choices: 45 features x 5 thresholds x 2 sides. Thresholds were Jan-Apr
      quantiles. The features cover open-interest acceleration and consecutive drops, ETH open interest, long/short
      ratios, top-trader account and position ratios (level, change, 7-day z-score), the taker ratio, funding,
      premium (last value, 60 min, 8 h, z-score), time of day, volatility regime, volume, depth imbalance and
      price moves.
  That is up to about 258 million settings, using maxpos 1. Definitions with fewer than 30 signals were skipped.
  On top of that: a 160,000-setting family check, neighbourhood tables, and explorations.

4. OTHER OBSERVATIONS
  * At honest timing, the open-interest drop, ETH open-interest drop, long/short and top-trader ratios, taker ratio,
    funding and premium all have rank correlations of at most about 0.1 with the next 5 to 60 minutes. Their signs
    flip between Jan-Feb, Mar-Apr and May-Jun.
  * Following open-interest drops with moves of 0.3 to 0.5 % loses steadily, because costs (about 2 spreads, around
    $36) exceed the leftover push. Only very large moves (at least 0.8 %) leave a short follow-through.
  * Fading a flush over 30 to 120 minutes produced big in-sample numbers, but no better than the fakes.

5. VERDICT (plain)
  Nothing honest from the open-interest / positioning angle clearly beats the luck check of my full search. Once the
  4-minute look-ahead is removed, most of the open-interest edge is gone.
  OI_BIGMOVE meets the build rules and beats the fakes inside the short-hold family. But that family was chosen after
  looking, about 75 % of its profit comes from February 2026, and Apr-Jun is flat (+1.9R; about 0 under stress).
  I would NOT trade it with real money at 10 % risk. Treat it as "luck check not passed" unless it holds up on the
  unseen months; at most, run it on paper.
